New · X-Deal

No budget? Trade food, products or a staycation for a video.

UGC is the cheapest way to get great content — and with X-Deal you don't even need cash. Clear steps, a countdown, and protection for both sides.

🎁 How an X-Deal works

  1. OfferFood, a product or a staycation (+ an optional tip). Some creators set a minimum tip.
  2. Creator accepts & sets the release timee.g. “video 5 days after I receive it”.
  3. Item sent → creator confirms receivedThe countdown starts — both of you see it.
  4. Watermarked 1080p previewApprove or ask 1 revision. Quiet for 3 days? Auto-approved.
  5. High-res on Drive → confirm → tip & rateLiked it? Send a tip.

🛡 Protection

  1. Valid ID for creatorsBusinesses send real items — every X-Deal creator is ID-verified.
  2. Trust limitsNew creators: items up to ₱800, ₱2,000 after 3 on-time X-Deals.
  3. Strikes7 days past due = strike. 3 strikes = no more X-Deals.
  4. TapTayo Protect (₱149/mo)Creator ghosted? The declared item value comes back as TapTayo credits (up to ₱1,500 a claim).
  5. Every step is loggedTimestamps, photos and tracking — your evidence.

Questions

Do I need an account to look for creators?

No. Search and browse freely. You only log in to make an offer, book, or apply to a collab.

How do payments work?

The client pays the creator directly (GCash, Maya, bank) — the downpayment locks the date, the balance is paid on delivery. TapTayo doesn't hold your money and takes 0% commission.

What's UGC?

User-generated content: videos and photos a creator makes for your brand, which you post on your own page or use in ads. Great content matters more than followers.

Can I book a creator who is also my customer?

Yes. One TapTayo account can be a client, a creator and a business at the same time — switch anytime in My TapTayo.

What if the creator is late?

Rush jobs have a countdown. If the deadline is missed, the client is compensated the way the creator set it: rush fee refunded, a discount next time, or both.

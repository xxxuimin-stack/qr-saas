1. Who we are

TapTayo (taptayo.com) is an online platform in the Philippines that lets small businesses take orders, bookings and reservations through QR codes and links. When we say "we", we mean the operator of TapTayo. The DTI or SEC name is not printed here yet. It will be added on this page after that registration is confirmed. When we say "you", we mean anyone who uses TapTayo — a customer, a business owner, a staff member, or a creator.

By creating an account or using TapTayo, you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you are under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission to use TapTayo, and some features (like stays and creator deals) are for adults only.

2. Accounts

  • A free TapTayo account is needed to book appointments, home service, stays and reservations, and to use X-Deal and collabs. You can log in with Google or email.
  • Some food shops allow quick guest orders without an account. The shop decides this.
  • Keep your login safe. You are responsible for what happens under your account and your staff PINs. Tell us right away if you think someone else is using it.
  • Give true information. Don't pretend to be someone else or a business you don't run.

3. For customers

  • You buy from the shop, not from TapTayo. The shop is responsible for its food, products, services, rooms, prices, and for honoring your booking.
  • Shop policies apply. Before you book, you'll see the shop's policy — downpayment, refund, rescheduling, house rules, late check-out fees. When you tick "I agree", that agreement is between you and the shop.
  • Payments. Most payments (cash, GCash, bank transfer) go straight to the shop. TapTayo does not hold that money and cannot reverse it. Keep your reference numbers.
  • Problems with a shop? Talk to the shop first. If you think a shop is a scam or unsafe, use "Report this shop" on its page. We review every report, but we can't promise a refund that only the shop can give.

4. For businesses

  • You must have the right to sell what you list, and follow Philippine law — including the Consumer Act, the Internet Transactions Act, food safety, permits, and taxes.
  • Show true prices and clear policies. Honor the bookings and orders you accept. Give refunds you promised, including when an item runs out after the customer has paid.
  • You are responsible for your staff and for what they do in TapTayo.
  • You handle your customers' personal information (names, phone numbers, IDs, addresses) for your business. Use it only to serve them, keep it private, and follow the Data Privacy Act. By using TapTayo you instruct us to process that information only to run orders, bookings, stays, and support. See the Privacy Policy.
  • New shops have safety limits for customers (for example, a ₱1,500 cap on stay downpayments) until the shop is verified.

5. For creators (X-Deal and collabs)

  • Deliver what you agreed to, on time, and make it your own original work. Mark paid or gifted posts as ads or partnerships where the platform or the law asks you to.
  • A business may ask for one revision. If it cancels after the revision, the deal ends and your creator trust score goes down by 1. You may appeal once; if we uphold your appeal, your score is restored and the cancel counts against the business.
  • Businesses that cancel often may be limited in how many deals they can send.
  • Both sides keep the right to post the content as agreed in the deal. Don't use the other side's content beyond what you agreed.

6. Plans and payments

  • Free stays free. Basic (₱99/month) and higher plans, add-ons and packs cost what the Billing page shows at the time you pay.
  • New businesses get Basic for 14 days at no cost. When it ends, your shop goes back to Free automatically. We won't charge you unless you choose a paid plan.
  • Paid plans run for the period you paid for. Unless the law requires it or we say otherwise, payments for a period you already started are not refundable.
  • Referral rewards count only when the shop you invited becomes active (at least 20 completed orders or bookings).
  • We may change prices for future periods. We'll tell you before a new price applies to you.

7. Receipts and taxes

TapTayo is not a BIR-accredited POS or cash register system. Digital receipts, order slips and reports from TapTayo are not official receipts or invoices. Businesses are responsible for issuing official receipts or invoices and paying their own taxes as required by the BIR.

8. Badges, reviews and reports

  • Badges like "Verified", "Active", "DTI checked" and "BIR checked" mean we checked certain things at a certain time, or that the shop has a record of real orders on TapTayo. A badge is not a guarantee of quality or that nothing will go wrong.
  • Documents you upload for verification (IDs, DTI, BIR) are private. They are seen only by the TapTayo team that checks them, and they are never shown on your public page.
  • Reviews must be honest and from real customers. Don't post fake reviews or pay for good ones.
  • Reports must be true. False reports to harm a business may lead us to limit your account.

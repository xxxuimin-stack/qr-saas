Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This is a plain-language draft that follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). We'll update it after legal review.

  • We collect only what's needed to run orders, bookings and stays.
  • Uploaded IDs and documents are private. They won't be seen, used, or downloaded by anyone other than the people who need them (the shop's staff, or the TapTayo team for verification). They are never shown publicly.
  • Private customer files are deleted automatically after 90 days by default.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

1. What we collect

When you order or book

  • Name, mobile number, and optional Messenger link
  • What you ordered or booked, date and time, notes and requests
  • For home service and delivery: address, landmark and map pin
  • For stays and some home services: valid ID photo, guest IDs, signature on the waiver
  • Payment details you enter, like a GCash reference number or a screenshot of payment (we never ask for card numbers or PINs)
  • Optional: birthday (for a birthday treat), referral and promo codes

When you create an account

  • Your email and name from Google or your email sign-up, and your login history

When you run a business or create content

  • Business name, address, contact numbers, menu and photos
  • Staff names and roles (staff PINs are stored scrambled)
  • Verification documents you choose to upload: owner ID, DTI, BIR
  • For creators: ID for age and identity checks, and your deal history

Automatically

  • Basic device and usage data (browser type, pages used, errors), and push notification tokens if you turn on alerts

2. How we use it

  • To place your order or booking and show it to the shop
  • To send updates you asked for — like "your order is ready", reminders and receipts
  • To keep people safe: verify shops and creators, prevent scams and fake bookings, handle reports and appeals
  • To run billing, fix problems, and improve TapTayo
  • To follow the law

We use your information because you asked for the service (a contract), because you agreed (for example, marketing messages or optional fields), because we have a legitimate interest in keeping TapTayo safe, or because the law requires it.

3. Shops and TapTayo: who is responsible

When you order or book with a shop, the shop decides why and how your information is used to serve you (they are the "personal information controller"). TapTayo processes it for them so the app works (we are their "personal information processor"). For your TapTayo account, verification, and safety features, TapTayo is the controller.

Shops must use your information only to serve you, keep it private, and follow the Data Privacy Act. If you have a question about how a shop used your information, you can ask the shop, or contact us and we'll help.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see what you uploaded.
  • The shop and its staff see what they need to serve you. Private uploads (IDs, waivers, payment screenshots) are opened only by the shop's staff through short-lived secure links.
  • The TapTayo team sees verification documents and reports only to check them, using secure links that expire in 15 minutes.
  • Service providers that help run TapTayo, under contract and only for that purpose: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (hosting and storage, Singapore region), Google Sign-In, SMS and push notification providers, and payment providers for TapTayo subscriptions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information. Your public shop or creator page shows only what you choose to make public — never your documents.

5. How long we keep it

  • Private customer files (IDs, guest IDs, waivers, payment screenshots): deleted automatically after 90 days by default. A shop may set a shorter period.
  • Order and booking records: kept while the shop's account is active, so you and the shop have a history and receipts.
  • Verification documents: kept while the business is verified, then deleted.
  • Account data: kept until you ask us to delete your account, except what the law requires us to keep.

6. How we protect it

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on Google Cloud with access rules.
  • Private files are stored in a locked folder that is never public. Links to them expire quickly.
  • Staff use their own PINs, and owners can remove staff any time.
  • If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell the people affected and the National Privacy Commission within the time its rules require for that incident. This page does not set a shorter promise.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object, to correct it, to have it deleted or blocked, to get a copy you can take elsewhere, to withdraw consent, and to claim damages. You can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use these rights, contact us (below). We'll reply within 15 working days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

8. Cookies and your device

TapTayo stores small bits of data on your phone or browser so it works — for example, your cart, your last order, your name and number so you don't type them again, and your login. We don't use advertising cookies. You can clear these anytime in your browser settings.

9. Children

TapTayo is not made for children under 13. Users under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission. Stays and creator deals are for adults only. If you think a child gave us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Contact our Data Protection Officer

Email privacy@taptayo.com, or message us through the Help page. This address is the privacy contact. Registration with the National Privacy Commission is a separate step. Under NPC Circular 2022-04 it can apply at 250 employees, at sensitive information of 1,000 people, or when the processing likely puts people's rights at risk. This page does not claim that TapTayo is already registered. We may update this policy; if a change is important, we'll tell account holders before it takes effect.

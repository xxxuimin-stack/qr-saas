Questions?

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They scan your QR or open your shop link in any phone browser.

Can I run everything from my phone?

Yes. A tablet at the counter is optional.

How do customers pay?

Cash, cash on delivery, or GCash and bank transfer. Your staff verify the reference numbers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit, with small-use limits. Upgrade when you need more staff, screens or features. Your menu and records are always kept.

Is TapTayo a BIR-accredited POS?

No. TapTayo receipts are digital order receipts, not official receipts or BIR invoices. Keep issuing your BIR invoices the way your registration requires.

I have more than one branch. Is that okay?

Yes. All your branches live under one account.

I'm a freelancer (nails, lashes, massage, makeup). How do I get jobs?

Open taptayo.com/pro, add your skills, your area and at least 10 photos of your work, then apply to shops hiring near you. When a shop accepts you, switch on “Available” — each job offer shows the area, the distance and what you earn, and you can decline.

How do you keep shops and clients safe?

Shops get verified badges from their permits (and can't rename once verified). Shops can block clients and report serious cases — TapTayo checks every report: 2 confirmed reports pause booking for 30 days, 3 close it. Clients can report or block a shop too.
